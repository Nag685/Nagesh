Case studies

Three pieces of work, written up the way I would report an incident: what happened, what I found, what I did.

Workday HRDW credential migration

Resolved · zero disruption
Situation
User IDs had to be rotated across more than 50 Workday HRDW integrations without interrupting service.
Finding
Updates made to the shared delivery service do not automatically reach each individual integration.
Action
I led the rotation and updated the affected integrations individually.
Result
The gap was caught before it caused a broader outage, and the migration finished with zero service disruption.

Recurring message-queue alert storm

Root cause found · escalated
Situation
A batch reporting queue kept triggering waves of alerts.
Action
I investigated the pattern, identified the trigger, and collected supporting evidence.
Result
Escalated to the messaging team with that evidence so they could apply a permanent fix rather than another workaround.
